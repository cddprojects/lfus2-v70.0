FAQ

Common questions

Is Cruriter an employer?

No. We operate a job alert and registration service. We connect interested candidates with role information shared by hiring companies. Employment, if offered, is directly with the hiring company.

Why is registration free?

Hiring companies may fund the alert service. There is never a fee for candidates to register or receive alerts.

Why is there a minimum age requirement?

Partner companies set eligibility requirements for digital-support roles. Our registration form currently accepts adults aged 21 and above.

What kind of notification will I get?

You may receive role descriptions, core requirements, compensation details when available, and application instructions when a relevant alert is sent.

Will I be paid through Cruriter?

No. Any pay, tasks, schedules, and payment methods are determined by the hiring company and explained during their hiring process.
